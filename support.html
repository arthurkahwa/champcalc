Support

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Champ Calc computes, at any point in an F1 season, what each driver or team still needs to win — or when they're mathematically eliminated. If something isn't working as expected, here's how to get help.

Common questions

  • Standings look out of date. Season data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes during a race weekend, and daily otherwise. Pull to refresh in the app to force an update, and check your internet connection if the data still looks stale.
  • A driver or team is missing, or points look wrong. Data comes from a public F1 results feed (Jolpica-F1). Please report the discrepancy using the contact details below and include the race/round in question.
  • Push notifications aren't arriving. Champ Calc sends at most one notification per completed race. Check that notifications are enabled for the app in iOS Settings, and see the Privacy Policy for what a notification does and doesn't send off your device.
