Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Champ Calc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no user data of any kind sent off your device.

What the app does

Champ Calc reads publicly available Formula 1 standings and calendar data from a static JSON file hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com/arthurkahwa/champcalc) and computes championship-contention math from it, entirely on your device. These requests carry no account identifiers, device identifiers, or user content — they're the same request anyone would get fetching a public web page.

What stays on your device

  • Preferences (e.g. onboarding completion, your selected driver/team) — stored locally via iOS's standard UserDefaults, never transmitted anywhere.
  • A local cache of the season data JSON, stored in the app's Caches directory, so the app still works offline or if the data source is briefly unavailable. Also never transmitted anywhere.

Neither of these ever leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Push notifications

If you opt in when prompted, Champ Calc sends at most one push notification per completed race (e.g. a title clinch or elimination). This requires your device to register an APNs device token, which is sent only to the app's own push-delivery function — never to any analytics, advertising, or third-party service — solely to address that notification to your device. Declining the permission prompt disables this entirely; every other feature of the app works the same either way.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or user-generated content.
  • No tracking, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children's privacy

Champ Calc does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. The only data point the app can send off-device — a push notification device token, and only if you opt in — is not linked to any identity and is used solely to deliver the notification described above.

Changes to this policy

If Champ Calc's data practices change, this page will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's App Store Connect privacy declarations.
